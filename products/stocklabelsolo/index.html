StockLabel Solo · ZEROSTONZ

Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026.

StockLabel Solo is designed to manage inventory on your Android device without an account or a StockLabel Solo cloud service.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the product information you enter or import, locations, inventory quantities and transaction history, label projects and output records. If you select a photo, the app stores a processed copy with the product. These records stay in app storage on your device unless you choose to export or back them up.

Camera and files

If you grant camera permission, the app uses the camera to read codes for inventory lookup. Camera recognition does not automatically record an inventory transaction. You can enter a code manually instead. You can choose CSV files and photos through Android's file picker and choose a destination for PDFs, exports and backups. If you select a cloud storage provider, that provider handles the chosen file under its own terms and privacy practices.

Collection and sharing

The current app does not include a StockLabel Solo account, cloud server, advertising, or analytics. It does not send your product data to ZEROSTONZ. Android and any file or printing app you choose may process data as part of their own services.

Backups and deletion

You control when and where to create a full backup. Removing the app normally removes its local app data. Export a full backup before uninstalling or changing devices if you want to retain that data. Files you independently saved elsewhere are managed through the storage provider you chose. A checked backup can replace the current local dataset; the app keeps a local recovery copy first.
