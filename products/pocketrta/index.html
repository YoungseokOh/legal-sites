ZEROSEOK · PocketRTA

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 25, 2026

This policy describes how PocketRTA: Audio Analyzer handles microphone audio, measurements, and files. The app is published by ZEROSEOK.

Microphone and audio

PocketRTA accesses the microphone only after you start a microphone-based feature and grant Android's microphone permission. Live analysis processes audio on your device in temporary memory; it is not automatically uploaded to ZEROSEOK. If you explicitly start a recording, the app saves a WAV file on your device. Background level logging, when started by you, stores level readings, durations, and diagnostics locally and does not save raw audio. You can stop it from the app's ongoing notification.

Measurements, sessions, and files

Projects, sessions, measurement arrays, notes, calibration profiles, and logs are stored on your device. PocketRTA does not require an account and does not include advertising or analytics services. Android automatic backup is disabled for app data. If you choose to export, back up, or share a file, Android passes it to the destination you select; that destination handles it under its own privacy practices.

App-managed data is removed when you delete it in the app or clear/uninstall app data. Copies exported elsewhere must be removed from those destinations separately.

Network and purchases

All features in the initial release are free. This release does not offer in-app purchases. Google Play may process data needed to distribute the app under Google's own privacy policy. If a later release adds purchases, this policy will be updated before those purchases are offered.

Children

PocketRTA is a general-purpose audio measurement tool. It is not designed for children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security and changes

Audio and measurement data remain in app-managed storage unless you choose to export or share them. Keep exported files in a location you trust. If the app's data practices change, this policy will be revised and its effective date updated.
