# RAIL ATLAS Privacy Policy

**Effective date: July 31, 2026**

Candidate canonical URL; publication is not yet claimed:
[https://youngseokoh.github.io/rail-atlas-privacy/](https://youngseokoh.github.io/rail-atlas-privacy/)

RAIL ATLAS is a local-first focus game published by Zero Stonz. This policy
explains what the app processes and where that information stays.

## Information processed on your device

When you explicitly choose **Use my location**, Android may provide a one-time
foreground location so RAIL ATLAS can suggest the nearest bundled rail station.
The location is used only for that suggestion. Raw coordinates and location
history are not stored.

The app stores your selected and current stations, focus task text, focus-session
timing, completed rail logs, train progress, Credits, XP, visited routes, and
settings in its private on-device storage.

## Optional anonymous usage analytics

Anonymous usage analytics is off by default. If you enable **Share anonymous
usage analytics** in Settings, RAIL ATLAS uses Google Analytics for Firebase
to record coarse events such as onboarding completion, journey start, journey
completion, cancellation, and Engine upgrades.

RAIL ATLAS never sends task text, station or route identifiers, raw location,
exact timestamps, or your local trip history. Google Analytics may process app
interactions, an app-installation identifier, device/app metadata, and
approximate location inferred from network information. Advertising-ID
collection and ad-personalization signals are disabled. You can turn analytics
off at any time in Settings.

## No account or journey backend

RAIL ATLAS has no account, advertising, cloud sync, or production journey
backend. The rail graph, map boundaries, and visual assets are bundled with the
app. RAIL ATLAS does not sell personal information. If analytics is enabled,
Google processes only the limited data described above as our service provider.

## Permissions

Location permission is optional and is requested only after you choose the
location action. You can deny it and select a starting station manually with the
same core functionality. RAIL ATLAS does not request background location.

## Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you use the Debug reset in a non-production build,
clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Android
cloud backup is disabled for RAIL ATLAS, so uninstalling the app removes its
private atlas.

## Third-party data

Bundled station, schedule, and route-shape data is derived from the official
Amtrak GTFS Schedule feed. Bundled state boundaries are derived from U.S.
Census Bureau TIGER/Line data. These sources are not contacted while you use
the app.

## Changes

If this policy changes materially, the updated policy will be included with an
app update and its effective date will change.
