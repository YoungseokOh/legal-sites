PRIVACY POLICY · SAIL ATLAS

Local-first
by design.

SAIL ATLAS is a local-first focus application. It does not require an account and does not operate a production backend.

Effective date: July 31, 2026

ACCOUNTNot required
ANALYTICSOptional · off by default
STORAGEOn device

Data used

  • Approximate or precise foreground location is requested only when you choose Use nearest supported harbor.
  • Location is used in memory to rank nearby bundled harbors. Raw coordinates and location history are not stored or transmitted.
  • Your selected harbor, focus sessions, completed routes, game progress, preferences, and vessel configuration are stored locally on your device.

Optional anonymous usage analytics

Anonymous usage analytics is off by default. If you enable Share anonymous usage analytics in Settings, SAIL ATLAS uses Google Analytics for Firebase to record coarse events such as onboarding completion, voyage start, voyage completion, cancellation, and rig upgrades.

SAIL ATLAS never sends task text, harbor or route identifiers, raw location, exact timestamps, or local voyage history. Google Analytics may process app interactions, an app-installation identifier, device/app metadata, and approximate location inferred from network information. Advertising-ID collection and ad-personalization signals are disabled. Analytics can be turned off at any time in Settings.

Data sharing

SAIL ATLAS does not sell personal data and contains no advertising, authentication, cloud synchronization, or third-party map SDK. If analytics is enabled, Google processes only the limited analytics data described above as our service provider.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains on the device until you reset app data, clear the application’s storage, or uninstall the application.

Children

SAIL ATLAS is a general productivity application and is not directed to children under 13.
