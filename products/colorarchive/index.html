English

Camera/photo sampling and saved colors

ColorArchive uses optional camera permission for live color sampling, or reads a photo that you select through the document picker. Camera frames and decoded photos are used on your device; the app does not record a camera video or save a selected photo in the palette library. It does not change or delete the selected original. Palette/color names, RGB values and acquisition/creation times are stored locally. A locked camera RGB value and its time, input drafts and dialog recovery state can be kept in Android saved state; the original image is not included.

Retention, editing and deletion

Live frame/photo previews are transient. They can remain through Activity rotation while the ViewModel lives; after process recreation a source photo must be selected again. Saved colors and palettes remain until you delete them in the app, clear app storage or uninstall. Deleting a palette deletes its associated saved colors. Editing a color changes that saved value without editing the source image.

CSV, PNG and clipboard

CSV export contains saved palette/color names, color values and stored times. PNG cards contain the color swatch and labels plus embedded metadata describing palette/name, RGB/HEX, color conversions and stored creation time; they do not include the original camera/photo image or its EXIF location metadata. Copy HEX places the selected color text on Android’s clipboard, where Android or permitted receiving apps may read it. Exported files are delivered only to the destination you select. The app does not provide CSV backup restoration.

Local diagnostics and optional reports

The app keeps bounded diagnostic events, coded outcomes, timestamps, app version and a random local installation identifier in app-private storage. These records are not automatically transmitted and do not include your saved content or precise coordinates. Help & feedback lets you export a diagnostic file to a location you choose; it does not send a report to ZEROSEOK. You can clear the local activity log in the app.

Network, providers and this website

The current app has no account, developer upload server, advertising, remote analytics or remote crash-reporting SDK, and does not request Android Internet permission. Choosing a document provider may cause that provider to download or upload a file under its own policy. Exported files remain at the destination you choose, and a provider or receiving app may process them under its own policy. Opening this privacy page uses your browser. This page is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub handles website request information under its own privacy policy. If you email us, we receive the address, message and attachments you choose to send, and use them to respond to your inquiry; your email provider also processes the message.

Storage, backup and deletion

Local data is kept within Android app-private storage; the Android sandbox restricts other apps’ access. The app does not add its own file encryption. Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup are disabled for app data. Clearing Android app storage or uninstalling removes the private data. Exported files and any files retained by another provider must be deleted separately from their chosen destination.

Contact and changes

ZEROSEOK operates this app. For privacy questions, contact seok7302@gmail.com. Changes to this policy will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.

카메라·사진 샘플링과 저장 색상

ColorArchive는 선택적 카메라 권한으로 실시간 색상을 샘플링하거나 문서 선택 화면에서 고른 사진을 읽습니다. 카메라 프레임과 읽은 사진은 기기에서 처리하며 카메라 영상을 녹화하거나 선택한 사진을 팔레트 보관함에 저장하지 않습니다. 원본을 변경·삭제하지 않습니다. 팔레트·색상 이름, RGB와 획득·생성 시각을 로컬에 저장합니다. 잠근 카메라 RGB와 시각, 입력 초안과 대화상자 복구 상태는 Android 저장 상태에 유지될 수 있지만 원본 이미지는 포함하지 않습니다.

보관·편집·삭제

실시간 프레임과 사진 미리보기는 임시 상태입니다. ViewModel이 유지되는 화면 회전 중에는 남을 수 있지만 프로세스가 재생성되면 사진을 다시 선택해야 합니다. 저장한 색상과 팔레트는 앱에서 삭제하거나 앱 저장 공간을 지우거나 앱을 삭제할 때까지 보관합니다. 팔레트를 삭제하면 연결된 색상도 삭제합니다. 색상 편집은 저장한 값을 변경하며 원본 이미지를 편집하지 않습니다.

CSV·PNG·클립보드

CSV에는 저장한 팔레트·색상 이름, 색상 값과 시각이 포함됩니다. PNG 카드에는 견본·라벨과 팔레트·이름, RGB·HEX, 색상 변환 수치, 저장 생성 시각을 설명하는 메타데이터가 포함됩니다. 원본 카메라·사진 이미지나 EXIF 위치 정보는 포함하지 않습니다. HEX 복사는 Android 클립보드에 색상 문자열을 올리며 Android나 접근이 허용된 수신 앱이 읽을 수 있습니다. 내보내기는 사용자가 선택한 위치로만 전달합니다. CSV 백업 복원 기능은 없습니다.
