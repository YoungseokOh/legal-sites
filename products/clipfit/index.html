Videos and generated copies

When you choose or share a video, ClipFit reads it through Android's system document or sharing interface and creates an app-private working copy. The original is not modified. Compression, metadata inspection, thumbnail creation, and output verification happen on the device.

Working copies and unfinished drafts may remain in app-private storage so an interrupted task can be recovered. ClipFit removes abandoned private media after approximately 24 hours and removes draft assets when you explicitly dismiss them. Android or the user may remove app data earlier.

When you tap Share copy, ClipFit creates a temporary cache copy and asks Android to show its share chooser. The receiving app you select handles that copy under its own privacy policy. When you tap Save copy, ClipFit writes the verified copy to Movies/ClipFit using Android MediaStore. Saved copies remain until you delete them.

Network and third-party services

ClipFit does not make application-initiated network requests. It does not include analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, cloud-upload, or tracking SDKs.

Permissions

ClipFit does not request broad access to your media library, camera, microphone, contacts, location, or notification runtime permission. Android foreground-service permissions are used only while a video compression job started by you is actively running.

Children

ClipFit is a general-purpose file utility and is not designed specifically for children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
