Important distinction: DyeCue does not upload your photo pixels to a DyeCue-operated server. The bundled MediaPipe SDK may send limited performance and usage metrics to Google when Hair AI is used.

Photos and camera

DyeCue accesses only a photo you select through Android's system picker or a photo you capture in the app. The selected image is copied into app-private storage, normalized for orientation, and used to create a hair-confidence mask and color preview. Camera analysis frames are processed in memory and are not saved; only the photo captured when you press the shutter becomes a project source.

On-device Hair AI

Hair segmentation uses the bundled MediaPipe Tasks HairSegmenter model on your device. Photo pixels, face regions, confidence masks, repair strokes, and color recipes are not sent to a DyeCue server. Before Hair AI first runs, DyeCue explains that MediaPipe may send SDK performance and usage metrics to Google. You may decline and use manual Hair Paint instead.

Data stored on your device

DyeCue stores the canonical source photo, its cryptographic hash and dimensions, the AI mask, manual add/remove operations, color settings, project metadata, preview caches, and the last export metadata in app-private no-backup storage. The app does not require an account and does not provide cloud project storage.

Saving and sharing

DyeCue creates a full-resolution export only when you request it. The temporary result is reopened and verified before it is published to Android MediaStore. A result is shared only when you open Android's share sheet, using a verified content URI. The receiving app handles the shared copy under its own privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Camera: requested only for the in-app camera flow.
  • Photos: selected through Android's system picker without broad read-media access.
  • Storage on Android 8 and 9: write access is limited to Android API 28 and below and is used only to publish a requested export.
  • Internet and network state: included by MediaPipe/DataTransport dependencies. DyeCue does not use these permissions to upload photo pixels.

SDK metrics and data safety

MediaPipe Tasks Vision and its Google DataTransport dependencies may process limited app interactions, diagnostics, other app-performance information, and a device or app identifier for SDK analytics. This data is transmitted using encrypted connections. DyeCue does not include advertising, Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, account, or app-specific analytics SDKs.

Deletion and retention

Projects remain in app-private storage until you delete them or clear/uninstall the app. Deleting a project removes its source, mask, previews, operations, and temporary output from the project directory using normal filesystem deletion; it is not forensic secure erasure. Images already saved to MediaStore remain visible media and must be deleted separately with your gallery or file app.

Children

DyeCue is intended for people aged 13 and older. It is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
