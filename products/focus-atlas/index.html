Data stored on your device

The app stores your selected airport, focus plans and tasks, completed flight history, route progress, rewards, aircraft progress, app settings, and optional Missions locally on your device. FOCUS ATLAS does not require an account and does not operate a production server for this data.

Location

If you choose Use my location, FOCUS ATLAS requests foreground location access once to suggest nearby starting airports. The app does not request background location and does not store your coordinates or location history. You can deny the permission and use globe selection or offline airport search instead.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules a local notification on your device for the planned flight completion time. Notification permission is optional and does not affect focus-session restoration or rewards.

Optional anonymous usage analytics

Anonymous usage analytics is off by default. If you enable Share anonymous usage analytics in Settings, FOCUS ATLAS uses Google Analytics for Firebase to record coarse product events such as onboarding completion, journey start, journey completion, cancellation, and Engine upgrades.

The app never sends focus-task text, Mission names, airport or route identifiers, raw location, exact timestamps, or your local flight history. Google Analytics may process app interactions, an app-installation identifier, device/app metadata, and approximate location inferred from network information. This information is used only to understand feature reliability and aggregate usage. Advertising-ID collection and ad-personalization signals are disabled. You can turn analytics off at any time in Settings.

Sharing and third parties

FOCUS ATLAS does not sell personal data or serve ads. If you opt in to anonymous analytics, Google processes the limited analytics data described above as our service provider. Airport and map data are bundled with the app. Exported journey backups are created only when you request them and are shared through the destination you select in the operating-system share sheet.

Deletion and backups

You can delete local app data from the Debug settings in development builds or by removing the app data through your operating system. A journey backup can be exported and restored from Settings. Uninstalling the app may remove local data unless it is preserved by an operating-system backup.

Children

FOCUS ATLAS is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect children’s personal information.
