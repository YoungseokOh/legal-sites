Privacy Policy

Effective October 8, 2026

Heisei is a camera application published by Youngseok Oh. This policy explains what the Android app processes and what is sent to service providers.

Photos and camera access

Ordinary camera captures, local effects, the Heisei Roll, and MY CCD are processed and stored on your device. Camera permission is used to take photos. Exported and shared copies are controlled by their destinations.

Optional AI Flash and film photo edits require an internet connection and your explicit consent. The selected image is uploaded through our authenticated Firebase/Google Cloud service and sent to OpenAI to produce the requested edit. We remove embedded image metadata before processing. AI results are stored privately and retrieved through authenticated requests; they are not published as a public gallery.

Uploaded inputs are removed after processing. Result access expires after seven days; storage lifecycle cleanup is configured after eight days as a fallback and physical deletion may take additional processing time. OpenAI's own service retention terms also apply.

Optional accounts and purchases

HEISEI+ and cloud features use Firebase Authentication. Authentication providers may process your email address, provider identity, and Firebase user ID. Ordinary local camera use does not require an account.

Google Play handles subscription checkout and payment details. Our server processes purchase tokens, product and order identifiers, subscription status, and an account binding to verify access and record photo-use credits. We do not receive your full payment-card details.

Server records include generation status, selected style, credit usage, provider request identifiers, token usage, and cost accounting. Identity claims used to prevent repeated trial grants are stored as keyed hashes.

Analytics

Release versions use Google Firebase Analytics to understand app reliability and feature use. Events may include screen views, camera permission outcome, shutter and capture result, selected camera or settings, and export or share outcome. Firebase may process app-instance, device, operating-system, and approximate technical information needed to provide analytics. Heisei does not request Android advertising identifiers.

Crash diagnostics

Release versions use Google Firebase Crashlytics. When a crash or diagnostic event occurs, Firebase may process crash traces, diagnostics, app version, device model, and operating-system information so that we can find and fix defects.

Service provider and transfers

Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics are services provided by Google. Their processing is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and applicable Firebase terms. Network traffic to Firebase is encrypted in transit.

What we do not do

  • We do not request contacts, precise location, or health information.
  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not show ads or use data for advertising.
  • We do not offer public photo sharing or general cloud photo backup.

Retention and your choices

App-local photos, settings, and MY CCD data remain until you delete them, clear Heisei's app data, or uninstall the app. Exported or shared copies must be removed from their destination separately. Firebase retains analytics and diagnostic data according to the configured Firebase retention settings and Google's service terms.

Request deletion of cloud account and associated data using the Data Deletion page. The current release's in-app account-data deletion action is not yet available; this support route remains available. We may retain minimum purchase, security, trial-abuse, and accounting records when needed, and explain relevant retention when responding. Deleting an account does not cancel a Google Play subscription.

Cloud transport uses HTTPS. Google Firebase/Cloud and OpenAI process data as service providers and may process it outside your country.

You can deny camera permission, change it later in Android Settings, and use Android and Google privacy controls where available.

Children

Heisei is not primarily directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app or its service providers change. The effective date above will identify the current version.
