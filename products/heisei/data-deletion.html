HEISEI CAMERA / SUPPORT

Delete your data

Ordinary camera photos are stored locally. Optional HEISEI+ and AI edits also use a Firebase account, subscription/credit records, and private cloud processing as described in our Privacy Policy.

Request cloud account and data deletion

Email seok7302@gmail.com with the subject "HEISEI account deletion" from the email address associated with your HEISEI account. Include whether you request account deletion, associated photo/job data deletion, or both. Do not send passwords, payment card details, or photos. We verify ownership before acting.

The current in-app account-data deletion action is unavailable; use this support request instead. We review requests and explain any minimum records retained for purchase accounting, security, or prevention of repeated trial claims. Cloud result access also expires after seven days.

Account or data deletion does not cancel billing. Cancel an active subscription separately in Google Play's Payments & subscriptions.

Remove app-local data

  1. Open Android Settings.
  2. Choose Apps, then Heisei.
  3. Choose Storage, then Clear data; or uninstall Heisei.

This removes Heisei's app-local photos, MY CCD identity, and settings from that device.

Remove exported or shared copies

Photos exported to system Photos or shared to another app are separate copies. Delete them from that destination and its trash or recently deleted folder.

Analytics and diagnostics

For a privacy request concerning Firebase analytics or diagnostics, contact seok7302@gmail.com with the app name, device platform, and enough context to understand the request. We may not be able to connect an analytics event to an individual.
