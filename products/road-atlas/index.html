PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy,
by design.

ROAD ATLAS is a local-first focus game. Your focus journey stays where it belongs: on your device.

◆

This policy explains what ROAD ATLAS processes, why it is needed, and where that information stays.

ACCOUNT
Not required
ANALYTICS
None
STORAGE
On device
01

Information processed on your device

When you explicitly choose Use my location, Android may provide a one-time foreground location so ROAD ATLAS can suggest the nearest bundled road stop. The location is used only for that suggestion. Raw coordinates and location history are not stored.

The app stores your selected and current stops, focus task text, focus-session timing, completed road logs, vehicle progress, Credits, XP, visited routes, and settings in its private on-device storage.

02

No server collection

ROAD ATLAS has no account, advertising, analytics, cloud sync, or production backend. The road graph, map boundaries, and visual assets are bundled with the app. ROAD ATLAS does not transmit your task text, progress, or location to Zero Stonz or sell personal information.

03

Permissions

Location permission is optional and is requested only after you choose the location action. You can deny it and select a starting stop manually with the same core functionality. ROAD ATLAS does not request background location.

04

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you use the Debug reset in a non-production build, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Android cloud backup is disabled for ROAD ATLAS, so uninstalling the app removes its private atlas.

05

Third-party data

Bundled road geometry is derived from OpenStreetMap and OSRM. Bundled national boundaries use Natural Earth. These sources are not contacted while you use the app.

06

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the updated policy will be included with an app update and its effective date will change.
