English

Mileage records and optional precise location

MileLogLite stores vehicle names, trip dates, start/end odometer readings, distance, business/personal classification and notes locally. Manual mileage entry does not need location. When you explicitly start a GPS session, the app requests precise location and runs a location foreground service with a persistent recording notification; notification permission is requested where Android requires it. The service can continue while the screen is off or the app is not visible until you stop it or Android interrupts it. The app does not request the separate background-location permission and does not automatically start tracking on boot.

GPS checkpoints and retention

During an unfinished session the app stores accumulated distance, the latest accepted coordinate anchor and timing/status information in its private database so you can resume, save or discard the session. It does not store a full route history. A local session-specific marker records whether valid reception was observed; it contains no coordinates. Resuming clears the previous coordinate anchor rather than estimating the missing interval. Saving or discarding clears the unfinished session and its reception marker; the saved trip contains odometer/distance values and a GPS status note, not coordinates. If the service is interrupted, its unfinished checkpoint can remain until you choose how to finish it.

Reports, backup and deletion

CSV and PDF reports contain the selected month’s mileage records and totals. JSON backup contains committed vehicles and trips, not the unfinished GPS session or coordinate anchor; restore merges those records. These files may contain names or notes you entered and are not encrypted by the app. You choose the file provider and destination. Delete saved trips in the app and discard unfinished sessions when needed; vehicle records may remain until app storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled.

Local diagnostics and optional reports

The app keeps bounded diagnostic events, coded outcomes, timestamps, app version and a random local installation identifier in app-private storage. These records are not automatically transmitted and do not include your saved content or precise coordinates. Help & feedback lets you export a diagnostic file to a location you choose; it does not send a report to ZEROSEOK. You can clear the local activity log in the app.

Network, providers and this website

The current app has no account, developer upload server, advertising, remote analytics or remote crash-reporting SDK, and does not request Android Internet permission. Choosing a document provider may cause that provider to download or upload a file under its own policy. Exported files remain at the destination you choose, and a provider or receiving app may process them under its own policy. Opening this privacy page uses your browser. This page is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub handles website request information under its own privacy policy. If you email us, we receive the address, message and attachments you choose to send, and use them to respond to your inquiry; your email provider also processes the message.

Storage, backup and deletion

Local data is kept within Android app-private storage; the Android sandbox restricts other apps’ access. The app does not add its own file encryption. Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup are disabled for app data. Clearing Android app storage or uninstalling removes the private data. Exported files and any files retained by another provider must be deleted separately from their chosen destination.

GPS 임시 기록과 보관

미완료 세션 중에는 이어서 기록하거나 저장·버리기를 할 수 있도록 누적 거리, 마지막으로 받아들인 좌표 기준점과 시각·상태를 내부 데이터베이스에 저장합니다. 전체 경로 이력은 저장하지 않습니다. 세션별 유효 수신 확인 표시는 로컬에 보관하며 좌표를 포함하지 않습니다. 재개 시 이전 좌표 기준점을 지우고 끊긴 구간의 거리를 추정하지 않습니다. 저장하거나 버리면 미완료 세션과 수신 표시를 제거합니다. 저장한 주행에는 계기판·거리 값과 GPS 상태 메모만 포함하고 좌표는 포함하지 않습니다. 서비스가 중단되면 사용자가 처리 방법을 선택할 때까지 미완료 기록이 남을 수 있습니다.
